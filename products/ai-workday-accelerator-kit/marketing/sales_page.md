# How It Works

### 1. Choose a Business System

Select the workflow that matches the professional task you need to complete.

### 2. Gather Your Information

Review the Required Input Information section and prepare the relevant context, data and constraints.

### 3. Follow the Professional AI Workflow

Use the structured workflow to guide the AI through the task.

### 4. Run the Professional AI Prompt

Provide the prepared information to your preferred AI assistant.

### 5. Review the Generated Output

Evaluate the result rather than accepting AI output blindly.

### 6. Validate

Use the included Validation Checklist to identify missing information, inconsistencies or potential issues.

### 7. Finalize

Apply your professional judgment and adapt the result to your organization.
